+How it works & limitations⌄

The model learns patterns from past sales and uses the selected forecast weather, holiday and nearby-event conditions. The forecast date is the day after the latest complete day in the data; it is not necessarily tomorrow on the calendar.

Sales during stockouts can undercount demand. Stockout flags should be recorded independently; flagged sales are excluded as fitting targets, but remain available as historical context. Missing or inaccurate flags and excluding these days can bias results.

This is a small gradient-boosted regression-tree model, not LightGBM. Forecasts are estimates, not guarantees. The planning choices apply 90%, 100% or 110% to the raw prediction and round to a nonnegative whole number; they are simple rules, not optimization.
